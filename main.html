Growing together

Falling in love

Our love story is long, beautiful and truly cinematic, but here is the abridged version. It is the journey of two destined hearts, brought together and ordained beyond all odds. What makes it even more special is that it started with friendship. Being friends first made it so seamless for us to be open, vulnerable and truly ourselves with one another. From those sincere conversations grew a deep and genuine bond. A bond built on love, care, respect, trust and understanding that never wavered. Through every season, we became each other’s safe space, each other’s peace, laughter and strength. And with time, it became beautifully clear that we don’t want to do life without each other. This is just the beginning of forever for us. Aramide and Busayo Forever — AB FOREVER 💞

The question

The proposal

It happened on a beautiful Thursday, perfectly planned in collaboration with friends and family. I told her I had a friend’s birthday party to attend and asked her to accompany me, otherwise she would definitely suspect something 🤣 The proposal setup was already arranged at my brother’s place. I also planned to use his brother’s car for the day. So after I picked her up to head for the birthday party, my brother’s wife called as planned 😁 saying she had forgotten a key in the car and needed it urgently. That was how we redirected to my brother’s house. Everyone was already inside and cars were parked where she couldn’t see them. I went inside to drop the requested key and came back, telling her that my brother would like her to say hello before we left and voila — there it was 💃🏼🤣 A shout of surprise and glowing lights of “WILL YOU MARRY ME?” beaming in the dimly lit room. The shock on her face was priceless 🥹 Trust my baby to show off — and she said a BIG YES!
